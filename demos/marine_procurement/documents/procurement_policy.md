# Blue Current Marine Procurement Policy

Revision 3.2, effective 15 February 2026. This fictional policy applies to maintenance spares purchased by Blue Current Marine Services for its coastal vessel fleet. Prices and stock positions in the accompanying catalogue are synthetic snapshots dated 20 February 2026.

## Supplier selection and approval
- Buyers must verify that the requested part matches the vessel's approved equipment specification before placing an order.
- Purchases up to USD 2,500 may be approved by a Fleet Maintenance Supervisor.
- Purchases from USD 2,500.01 through USD 15,000 require approval from both the Fleet Maintenance Supervisor and Procurement Manager.
- Purchases above USD 15,000 require the Procurement Director's written approval before a purchase order is issued.
- Buyers should obtain two comparable quotations for purchases above USD 5,000 when two qualified suppliers exist.
- A single-source justification is required when only one qualified supplier can meet the required specification or delivery window.
- Supplier onboarding requires a completed compliance form, bank verification, tax registration, and sanctions screening.

## Catalogue, stock, and lead time
- The ship-spares catalogue is a planning snapshot and does not reserve inventory for a buyer.
- Catalogue quantity is reported in the supplier's stated sales unit; a kit may contain multiple pieces as specified in the product description.
- Availability must be reconfirmed with the supplier before committing a vessel schedule.
- Listed lead time is the supplier's estimate in business days after receipt of a valid purchase order.
- A blank availability value means the catalogue does not provide a stock quantity and must not be interpreted as zero or as available.
- Substitute parts require written approval from the vessel Chief Engineer and the Fleet Maintenance Supervisor.
- Buyers must preserve the product code, unit of measure, currency, and source date when comparing catalogue entries.

## Pricing, freight, and taxes
- Catalogue unit prices are in USD and exclude freight, customs duties, and applicable taxes unless a row explicitly says otherwise.
- A quoted price is valid only for its stated validity period; catalogue prices are not binding supplier offers.
- Procurement must compare total landed cost when freight or customs charges differ between qualified offers.
- Currency conversion must use the Finance team's published monthly rate on the purchase order approval date.
- No buyer may promise a supplier payment date before Finance confirms invoice acceptance.
- Urgent-order fees require Procurement Manager approval regardless of the underlying item price.

## Receiving and inspection
- The receiving team must check part number, quantity, visible damage, and supplied certificates against the purchase order.
- Critical safety equipment requires a certificate of conformity and batch traceability before it is released to a vessel.
- A discrepancy must be recorded within two business days of receipt and sent to Procurement with photographs where practical.
- Items with a rejected inspection status must be quarantined and must not be installed.
- Catalogue descriptions do not replace the vessel's controlled engineering drawings or safety procedures.

## Returns and records
- Return authorization must be obtained from the supplier before a spare is shipped back.
- Customized or installed items may be non-returnable; the purchase order terms govern the specific transaction.
- Buyers retain quotations, approvals, purchase orders, and receiving records for seven years.
- Procurement records must not disclose supplier banking details in general chat answers.
- This synthetic policy is for software demonstration only; it is not an actual maritime purchasing standard.
