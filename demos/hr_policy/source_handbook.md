# Northstar Tidal Systems Employee Handbook

Effective date: 1 January 2026. These fictional policies apply to regular full-time employees in India. Part-time and fixed-term arrangements are governed by their signed agreements where those differ.

## Annual leave
- Regular full-time employees receive 18 working days of annual leave per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per completed month.
- Employees should submit annual leave requests in the People Portal at least five working days before the first day away.
- Managers approve requests based on team coverage and respond within three working days.
- Up to five unused annual leave days may be carried into the next calendar year.
- Carried days expire on 31 March of the following year if they are not used.
- Annual leave is counted in working days and excludes company holidays and scheduled weekly rest days.
- A medical absence lasting more than two consecutive working days requires a certificate from a registered medical practitioner.

## Sick leave
- Regular full-time employees receive 10 paid sick days each calendar year.
- An employee should notify their manager before their scheduled start time when illness prevents attendance.
- Unused sick leave does not carry into the next calendar year and is not paid out at separation.
- Sick leave beyond the annual balance requires People Operations review before it is approved as paid leave.

## Attendance and working hours
- Standard working hours are 9:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Monday through Friday, including a 30-minute unpaid meal break.
- Employees must record attendance in the People Portal when they begin work and when they finish.
- Employees running more than 15 minutes late should message their manager as soon as they know of the delay.
- Three unexcused late arrivals in a calendar month trigger a manager check-in and coaching conversation.
- A missed attendance record should be corrected in the People Portal within two working days.

## Health and wellbeing benefits
- The company provides group medical insurance with a base annual cover of INR 500,000 for the employee.
- Eligible employees may add a spouse and up to two dependent children during the annual enrollment window in November.
- The employer pays the full employee premium; dependent coverage premiums are paid by the employee through payroll deduction.
- The wellbeing allowance reimburses up to INR 12,000 per calendar year for eligible fitness or counselling expenses.
- Wellbeing allowance claims require a dated receipt and must be filed within 30 days of purchase.
- This handbook does not specify maternity benefit amounts or parental leave duration; employees should contact People Operations for current statutory guidance.

## Remote and hybrid work
- Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with their manager's written approval.
- Remote work days should be recorded on the shared team calendar by 5 p.m. on the preceding working day.
- Employees must use company-managed devices and the approved secure connection when accessing internal systems remotely.
- Roles handling sealed inventory or physical equipment may be designated onsite and are not eligible for routine remote days.
- A temporary remote-work exception of up to ten working days may be requested from the department head for a documented circumstance.
- The company does not reimburse home internet or personal furniture costs under this policy.

## Business travel and reimbursement
- A manager must approve a domestic business trip before tickets or lodging are booked.
- Economy-class rail or air travel is reimbursable for approved domestic trips.
- Lodging is reimbursed up to INR 6,000 per night outside metro cities and INR 8,000 per night in metro cities.
- Local ground transport is reimbursed at actual cost when supported by a receipt.
- Employees must submit a completed travel expense claim within ten calendar days after returning.
- Meal reimbursement is capped at INR 900 per full travel day and INR 450 per partial travel day.
- Personal sightseeing, minibar purchases, and alcohol are not reimbursable business expenses.

## General expense claims
- Business expenses require manager approval before purchase when the expected cost exceeds INR 5,000.
- An expense claim must include the business purpose, cost center, payment date, and an itemized receipt.
- Employees should submit claims in the People Portal within 30 calendar days of the transaction.
- Claims missing required receipts are returned for correction and may be denied if not corrected within ten working days.
- Reimbursement is paid in the next monthly payroll cycle after Finance approves a complete claim.
- This handbook does not define relocation assistance or education reimbursement; those benefits are not established by this document.

## Questions and policy conflicts
- People Operations owns interpretation of this handbook and can be reached at peopleops@northstar.example.
- If a signed employment agreement conflicts with this handbook, People Operations reviews the conflict with the employee.
- This synthetic handbook is for software demonstration only and is not real employment advice.
