# Northstar Tidal Systems Employee Handbook

Effective date: 1 January 2026. These fictional policies apply to regular full-time employees in India. Part-time and fixed-term arrangements are governed by their signed agreements where those differ.

## Annual leave
- Regular full-time employees receive 18 working days of annual leave per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per completed month.
- Employees should submit annual leave requests in the People Portal at least five working days before the first day away.
- Managers approve requests based on team coverage and respond within three working days.
- Up to five unused annual leave days may be carried into the next calendar year.
- Carried days expire on 31 March of the following year if they are not used.
- Annual leave is counted in working days and excludes company holidays and scheduled weekly rest days.
- A medical absence lasting more than two consecutive working days requires a certificate from a registered medical practitioner.

## Sick leave
- Regular full-time employees receive 10 paid sick days each calendar year.
- An employee should notify their manager before their scheduled start time when illness prevents attendance.
- Unused sick leave does not carry into the next calendar year and is not paid out at separation.
- Sick leave beyond the annual balance requires People Operations review before it is approved as paid leave.

## Attendance and working hours
- Standard working hours are 9:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Monday through Friday, including a 30-minute unpaid meal break.
- Employees must record attendance in the People Portal when they begin work and when they finish.
- Employees running more than 15 minutes late should message their manager as soon as they know of the delay.
- Three unexcused late arrivals in a calendar month trigger a manager check-in and coaching conversation.
- A missed attendance record should be corrected in the People Portal within two working days.

## Questions and policy conflicts
- People Operations owns interpretation of this handbook and can be reached at peopleops@northstar.example.
- If a signed employment agreement conflicts with this handbook, People Operations reviews the conflict with the employee.
- This synthetic handbook is for software demonstration only and is not real employment advice.
