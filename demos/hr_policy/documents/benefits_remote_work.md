# Benefits and Remote Work Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026. These fictional policies apply to regular full-time employees in India. Part-time and fixed-term arrangements are governed by their signed agreements where those differ.

## Health and wellbeing benefits
- The company provides group medical insurance with a base annual cover of INR 500,000 for the employee.
- Eligible employees may add a spouse and up to two dependent children during the annual enrollment window in November.
- The employer pays the full employee premium; dependent coverage premiums are paid by the employee through payroll deduction.
- The wellbeing allowance reimburses up to INR 12,000 per calendar year for eligible fitness or counselling expenses.
- Wellbeing allowance claims require a dated receipt and must be filed within 30 days of purchase.
- This handbook does not specify maternity benefit amounts or parental leave duration; employees should contact People Operations for current statutory guidance.

## Remote and hybrid work
- Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with their manager's written approval.
- Remote work days should be recorded on the shared team calendar by 5 p.m. on the preceding working day.
- Employees must use company-managed devices and the approved secure connection when accessing internal systems remotely.
- Roles handling sealed inventory or physical equipment may be designated onsite and are not eligible for routine remote days.
- A temporary remote-work exception of up to ten working days may be requested from the department head for a documented circumstance.
- The company does not reimburse home internet or personal furniture costs under this policy.
