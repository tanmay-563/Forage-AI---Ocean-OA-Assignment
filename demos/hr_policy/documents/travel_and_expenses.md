# Travel and Expense Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026. These fictional policies apply to regular full-time employees in India. Part-time and fixed-term arrangements are governed by their signed agreements where those differ.

## Business travel and reimbursement
- A manager must approve a domestic business trip before tickets or lodging are booked.
- Economy-class rail or air travel is reimbursable for approved domestic trips.
- Lodging is reimbursed up to INR 6,000 per night outside metro cities and INR 8,000 per night in metro cities.
- Local ground transport is reimbursed at actual cost when supported by a receipt.
- Employees must submit a completed travel expense claim within ten calendar days after returning.
- Meal reimbursement is capped at INR 900 per full travel day and INR 450 per partial travel day.
- Personal sightseeing, minibar purchases, and alcohol are not reimbursable business expenses.

## General expense claims
- Business expenses require manager approval before purchase when the expected cost exceeds INR 5,000.
- An expense claim must include the business purpose, cost center, payment date, and an itemized receipt.
- Employees should submit claims in the People Portal within 30 calendar days of the transaction.
- Claims missing required receipts are returned for correction and may be denied if not corrected within ten working days.
- Reimbursement is paid in the next monthly payroll cycle after Finance approves a complete claim.
- This handbook does not define relocation assistance or education reimbursement; those benefits are not established by this document.
