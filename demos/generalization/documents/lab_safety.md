# Meridian University Materials Laboratory Safety Guide

Revision 1.4, effective 4 September 2026. This fictional guide applies to supervised undergraduate work in the Meridian University materials laboratory.

## Access and training
- Students may enter the laboratory only after completing the university's chemical hygiene and equipment orientation.
- A trained supervisor must be present whenever a student handles corrosive chemicals or operates powered cutting equipment.
- Visitors must sign the laboratory log and remain with their host for the entire visit.
- Laboratory doors must remain closed while chemical experiments are in progress.
- Food, drink, and personal headphones are not permitted at laboratory benches.

## Personal protective equipment
- Safety glasses with side shields are required whenever chemicals or powered tools are in use.
- A laboratory coat and closed-toe shoes are required in wet-chemistry work areas.
- Chemical-resistant gloves must be selected from the substance-specific glove chart rather than chosen by colour.
- Reusable gloves must be inspected before use and discarded if cracked, swollen, or contaminated inside.
- Face shields supplement safety glasses during splash-prone transfers but do not replace them.

## Chemical handling and storage
- Every secondary chemical container must show the chemical name, concentration, preparation date, and preparer initials.
- Incompatible chemicals must be stored in separate labelled cabinets according to the posted compatibility chart.
- Corrosive liquids must be carried in a secondary containment tray when moved between rooms.
- Unlabelled containers must not be opened and should be isolated for the laboratory manager.
- Students must review the current safety data sheet before using an unfamiliar substance.
- The guide does not specify medical treatment doses; exposures require immediate contact with campus emergency services.

## Equipment, waste, and incidents
- Powered saws may be operated only after the student passes the equipment-specific practical check.
- Machine guards must be in place before powered cutting begins.
- Broken glass must be placed in the marked rigid glass-waste container, never in ordinary trash.
- Chemical waste must be labelled with contents and date and placed in the designated waste cabinet.
- Spills larger than 100 millilitres or involving an unknown chemical require evacuation and a call to campus emergency services.
- All injuries, near misses, and equipment failures must be reported to the laboratory manager before leaving that day.
- This synthetic guide is for software generalization testing and is not actual safety advice.
