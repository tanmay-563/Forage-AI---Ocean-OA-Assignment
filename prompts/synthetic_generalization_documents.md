# Synthetic university laboratory safety generalization prompt

Create a fictional university materials laboratory safety guide with a revision date, scope, and student audience. Cover access/training, supervision, personal protective equipment, chemical labels and storage, powered equipment, waste, spill thresholds, and incident reporting. Use concrete, internally consistent rules. Explicitly state information the guide does not provide, such as emergency medical treatment doses, and refer users to campus emergency services rather than inventing instructions. Label the guide as synthetic and not actual safety advice. Use useful complete rules rather than generic filler.

The committed guide in `demos/generalization/documents/lab_safety.md` was authored to these constraints. This separate domain is only a generalization check; it does not replace either required demo.
